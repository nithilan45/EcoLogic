PAPER A — ICAART 2027 (CORE B conference)
Dir: papers/icaart/
RQ: Does looking at the query beat a cost-matched query-independent policy,
    and can an audit protocol distinguish routers that earn their complexity
    from those that do not?
PRIMARY AXIS: measured USD + accuracy (McNemar, Wilson CIs).
UNIQUE EXPERIMENTS (must appear as main results):
  - RouteLLM on its own GSM8K data, cost-matched mixture vs call-fraction matching
    (n=1307, stage7_10/s9_static_baselines.json, woais_experiments RouteLLM tables)
  - Pre-registered learned router + ceiling AUC (logistic vs GB/RF/kNN)
  - Temp-0 regeneration noise / variance decomposition
  - Exact naive-vs-true cost identity as an EVALUATION bias, illustrated on
    RouteLLM (not as a green-SE story)
FORBIDDEN (do not put in abstract, title, or Table 1):
  - Modelled joules as the headline metric
  - Wall-clock latency p50/p90, serverless DES, queueing
  - "Green software" / sustainability architecture language
  - Energy rate ±5× sweep as a main experiment
FORMAT: SciTePress, double-blind, 10k–50k characters excluding whitespace
        (target ~12 pages / ~25–40k chars). Regular research paper.
TITLE DIRECTION: "Does Looking at the Query Help? An Audit Protocol for LLM Routers"

              as a measurement-method result for sustainability reporting.
